- Uncommon: 28%
- Rare: 10%
- Legendary: 2%

- Common x1
- Uncommon x2.2
- Rare x5
- Legendary x12

## Animal income

Tier multipliers:
- Common x1
- Uncommon x4
- Rare x25
- Legendary x200

Size multipliers:
- Tiny x1
- Small x2
- Normal x5
- Large x15
- Huge x60
- Giant x300

Zone income growth is strongly exponential, approximately x4.257 per zone before other multipliers.

## Fusion

Fusion consumes exactly 3 compatible pets.
Base output size weights:
- Tiny 30
- Small 30
- Normal 22
- Large 11
- Huge 5
- Giant 2

Fusion cost is approximately 600 seconds of source-pet income.

## Selling

Approximate values:
- Pet sale: ~100 seconds of pet income
- Egg sale: ~40 seconds of corresponding income

## Hatch

Base:
`20 seconds × size × tier × zone growth ÷ growth modifiers`

Night hatch multiplier: x30.
